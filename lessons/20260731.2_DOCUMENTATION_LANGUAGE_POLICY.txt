Policy

The word "shall" is prohibited in STN-LABZ governance, engineering, standards, procedures, and operational documentation because it introduces ambiguity.

The normative verb "will" will be used consistently throughout STN-LABZ policies, standards, doctrine, procedures, engineering documentation, and operational documentation.
