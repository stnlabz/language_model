Scope: STN-LABZ-managed systems

Policy

FileZilla is not approved for installation or operational use on STN-LABZ-managed systems.

Basis

During the August 2026 FTPS incident, FileZilla 3.70.6 repeatedly failed to complete an explicit TLS connection from the affected workstation.

Independent testing established that the hosting service was operational, and an alternate FTP client successfully completed authenticated file transfers from the affected environment.

The available evidence therefore did not establish the hosting service as the cause of the operational failure.

Approved Client

WinSCP is approved for STN-LABZ operational use, subject to continued operational reliability and compliance with applicable STN-LABZ security and configuration requirements.

Approval of WinSCP does not exempt the software from future review, testing, restriction, or replacement when operational evidence warrants such action.

Engineering Principle

Software used for operational infrastructure must demonstrate reliable execution of its assigned function.

Failure of an application in a particular environment does not establish a general defect in that application. This restriction reflects documented STN-LABZ operational experience, observed behavior, and risk-management requirements.

Exception

FileZilla may be installed or executed within an isolated test environment when required for compatibility testing, reproduction of an incident, security research, comparative testing, or other authorized engineering investigation.

Testing authorization does not constitute approval for operational use.

Review

This policy may be reconsidered when new evidence demonstrates that the conditions responsible for the August 2026 incident have been identified and that FileZilla can reliably satisfy STN-LABZ operational requirements.

Until such evidence exists, FileZilla will remain unapproved for operational deployment.
