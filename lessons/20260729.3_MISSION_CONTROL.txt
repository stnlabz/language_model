Policy

The STN-LABZ Chaos MVC will be converted into the Architect MVC for Mission Control purposes.

Role

The Architect MVC will serve as the Mission Control Platform for all subordinate systems in the STN-LABZ ecosystem.

Responsibilities

Each department has its own work environment:

- Operator Interface: Tier 1 Department Operators.
- System Management: Tier 2 for Systems.
- Authority Management: Tier 3 Policy Management.
- Integrations: Digit, Rictus, Ezra.
