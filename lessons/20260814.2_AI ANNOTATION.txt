Policy

Any source code written or modified by an AI system will be annotated using the approved STN-LABZ AI annotation format.

This requirement applies to all development languages used by STN-LABZ.

The programming language's appropriate comment syntax will contain the annotation. The required annotation content and structure will remain consistent regardless of language.

AI Annotation Format

The required logical format is:

text
[AI:MODEL_NAME | YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC]

<AI-created or AI-modified code>

[End AI:MODEL_NAME]


When expressed using a C-style block comment syntax:

text
/*
 * [AI:MODEL_NAME | YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC]
 */

<AI-created or AI-modified code>

/* [End AI:MODEL_NAME] */


Equivalent native comment syntax will be used for languages that do not support C-style block comments.

Required AI Annotation Fields

Every AI annotation will identify:

- The AI model responsible for the modification.
- The UTC date and time of the modification.
- The beginning of the AI-created or AI-modified code.
- The end of the AI-created or AI-modified code.

The timestamp format will be:

text
YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC


Human Approval Annotation

AI modifications applied to architectural components require an accompanying human approval annotation.

The required logical format is:

text
[AI:MODEL_NAME | YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC]
[HUMAN: REVIEWER | YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC | APPROVE/DENY]

<AI-created or AI-modified code>

[End AI:MODEL_NAME]


The human annotation will identify:

- The human reviewer.
- The UTC date and time of review.
- The review decision as APPROVE or DENY.

Architectural Components

An AI annotation establishes modification provenance. It does not establish human approval.

AI-created or AI-modified architectural code requires explicit human review.

Where human approval is required, the applicable code will not be treated as approved until the required human annotation records an APPROVE decision.

A DENY decision will not be represented or interpreted as approval.

Annotation Integrity

AI and human annotations are engineering records and will accurately represent modification and review history.

Annotations will not be:

- Falsified.
- Backdated.
- Removed to conceal AI involvement.
- Altered to misrepresent the responsible AI model.
- Altered to misrepresent human review.
- Used to imply approval that was not granted.

Unknown attribution or approval information will not be guessed.

Language Independence

This policy governs annotation content, not programming-language comment syntax.

Each development language will use its appropriate native comment mechanism while preserving the required STN-LABZ annotation fields and modification boundaries.

Language-specific coding standards may define the exact syntax necessary to implement this policy.

They will not remove or weaken the underlying annotation requirement.

Applicability

This policy applies to all STN-LABZ development languages, repositories, projects, agents, modules, applications, services, libraries, tools, and other software assets where source-code annotation is technically supported.

Project-specific development standards may impose additional annotation requirements.

Purpose

This policy establishes direct source-level traceability and accountability for AI-assisted software development.

It preserves a durable distinction between:

- AI modification provenance.
- Human architectural review.
- Human approval authority.

Source-control history may provide additional provenance, but it does not replace annotations required by this policy.
