Purpose

STN-LABZ will implement an intelligence pipeline beginning with external information aggregation and progressing through investigation, controlled document creation, Trust Chain processing, corpus construction, and agent knowledge updates.

The pipeline provides a continuous engineering path from discovered intelligence to validated knowledge available to authorized STN-LABZ agents.

Intelligence Pipeline

The governing intelligence progression is:

External Information
        ↓
Rictus Intelligence Collection
        ↓
INT Record
        ↓
Human Investigation
        ↓
Controlled Markdown
        ↓
Chain
        ↓
rag_builder
        ↓
Validated Corpus Data
        ↓
kb.corpus.json
        ↓
Agent Corpus Validation
        ↓
Agent Knowledge Update

Each intelligence item retains an identifiable relationship with the information and artifacts produced from it throughout the pipeline.

Intelligence Collection

Rictus will aggregate information from approved external sources according to its assigned intelligence mission.

Information identified for tracking or further investigation will receive an intelligence identifier in the form:

INT-#####

The intelligence identifier provides the persistent operational reference for the intelligence item.

The intelligence record will preserve information required for continued investigation and processing, including applicable:

- Intelligence identifier.
- Source.
- Source reference.
- Discovery information.
- Intelligence summary.
- Supporting information.
- Processing state.
- Associated pipeline artifacts.

Rictus intelligence collection will continue to operate according to the established Rictus mission and intelligence requirements.

Human Investigation

An INT record may be retrieved through the Rictus IRC interface for human investigation.

The operator may use the intelligence identifier to retrieve the individual intelligence item and its associated source information.

Investigation may include review of:

- Original reporting.
- Primary sources.
- Supporting sources.
- Technical documentation.
- Related intelligence.
- Existing STN-LABZ research.
- Contrary evidence.
- Engineering relevance.

The intelligence record remains the common identifier connecting the investigation to subsequent pipeline activity.

IRC Pipeline Commands

Rictus will provide IRC commands for operator interaction with intelligence records and the intelligence pipeline.

The initial command relationship is:

| Command | Application | Operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| !show-ID | Rictus | Display the identified intelligence record |
| !md-ID | Rictus | Create the applicable Markdown intelligence artifact |
| !chain-ID | Rictus → Chain | Process the identified artifact through Chain |
| !rag-ID | Rictus → rag_builder | Process the identified artifact through rag_builder |

The INT identifier supplied through the command identifies the intelligence item being processed.

Rictus will preserve the relationship between the command, intelligence record, resulting artifact, and processing result.

Additional commands may be introduced as pipeline requirements evolve.

Operator Authentication

IRC pipeline commands are a human control mechanism.

Pipeline commands will be accepted only from an operator authenticated according to the applicable Rictus authentication requirements.

Command activity will preserve sufficient operational evidence to identify the authenticated operator, requested operation, intelligence identifier, processing result, and applicable time of execution.

Controlled Markdown

An intelligence item selected for continued processing will be represented as an applicable controlled Markdown artifact.

The artifact will preserve the INT identifier and applicable source provenance.

Controlled Markdown produced from intelligence will comply with established STN-LABZ Engineering Documentation requirements.

The relationship is:

INT-#####
        ↓
Investigation
        ↓
Controlled Markdown Artifact
        ↓
Document Identity
        ↓
Source Provenance

The controlled artifact becomes the document representation used by subsequent pipeline stages.

Chain Processing

The controlled Markdown artifact will be processed through Chain according to the STN-LABZ Trust Chain.

Chain will perform the applicable controlled-document and Trust Chain operations established by policy.

The INT record will retain the resulting Chain state and associated controlled-document identity.

The relationship is:

INT-#####
        ↓
Controlled Markdown
        ↓
Chain
        ↓
Root Document ID / Revision ID
        ↓
Trust Chain Result

Only artifacts satisfying the applicable Trust Chain requirements proceed to corpus processing.

rag_builder Processing

After successful applicable Trust Chain processing, the controlled artifact may proceed through rag_builder.

rag_builder will transform the controlled source material into the applicable validated corpus representation according to its established architecture and qualification requirements.

The pipeline relationship is:

Controlled Artifact
        ↓
Trust Chain PASS
        ↓
rag_builder
        ↓
Validated Corpus Artifact

Applicable provenance will remain associated with the resulting corpus data.

Knowledge Corpus

Validated corpus artifacts will be combined into the STN-LABZ knowledge corpus:

kb.corpus.json

The corpus aggregation process will construct kb.corpus.json from the applicable validated corpus artifacts.

Corpus construction will preserve the relationship between corpus knowledge and its authoritative controlled source.

The corpus will be constructed deterministically from the current applicable validated knowledge state.

The relationship is:

Validated Corpus Artifacts
        ↓
Corpus Aggregation
        ↓
kb.corpus.json

Corpus Updates

New intelligence may produce new or revised controlled knowledge.

When validated corpus data changes, the pipeline will rebuild kb.corpus.json from the applicable validated corpus artifacts.

The update progression is:

New or Revised Intelligence
        ↓
Investigation
        ↓
Controlled Artifact
        ↓
Chain
        ↓
rag_builder
        ↓
Validated Corpus Data
        ↓
Rebuild kb.corpus.json

Corpus construction will prevent duplicate knowledge from being introduced when the applicable data has already been represented in the authoritative corpus state.

Agent Corpus Validation

Agents using the STN-LABZ knowledge corpus will check for an updated corpus twice daily.

When an updated corpus is identified, the agent will validate the corpus according to its applicable knowledge and Trust Chain requirements before updating its operational knowledge state.

The progression is:

Scheduled Corpus Check
        ↓
Identify Current kb.corpus.json
        ↓
Validate Corpus
        ↓
Update Agent Knowledge State

The corpus is the authoritative aggregate supplied to the agent for knowledge indexing.

Agents will use the validated corpus rather than independently traversing the underlying controlled-document directory for operational knowledge construction.

Rebuild Model

Agent knowledge state is derived from the validated knowledge corpus.

When the authoritative corpus is updated, the applicable agent knowledge state will be rebuilt or re-indexed from the validated kb.corpus.json.

The governing relationship is:

Controlled Knowledge
        ↓
Validated Corpus Artifacts
        ↓
kb.corpus.json
        ↓
Agent Validation
        ↓
Agent Knowledge Index

This provides a repeatable path for reconstructing agent knowledge from the authoritative corpus.

Provenance

Provenance will remain traceable throughout the intelligence pipeline.

The intended provenance chain is:

External Source
        ↓
INT-#####
        ↓
Controlled Markdown
        ↓
Root Document ID / Revision ID
        ↓
Trust Chain Evidence
        ↓
rag_builder Corpus Artifact
        ↓
kb.corpus.json
        ↓
Agent Knowledge

Pipeline records will preserve sufficient information to trace agent knowledge back through the controlled corpus to the originating intelligence and source material.

Pipeline State

The INT record will provide a common operational location for tracking progression of an intelligence item through the pipeline.

Applicable pipeline state may include:

- DISCOVERED
- INVESTIGATING
- DOCUMENTED
- CHAINED
- CORPUS_BUILT
- PUBLISHED
- FAILED

Pipeline state will reflect completed engineering activity and associated evidence.

Failures will remain associated with the affected INT record for investigation and corrective action.

Continuing Intelligence Cycle

The intelligence pipeline is continuous.

New external information may create new intelligence records.

Existing intelligence may receive additional evidence.

Investigation may result in new or revised controlled artifacts.

Validated artifacts may produce updated corpus data.

Updated corpus data may produce a new kb.corpus.json.

Agents will periodically validate the available corpus and update their knowledge state when an applicable validated corpus update exists.

The continuing relationship is:

Collect
        ↓
Identify
        ↓
Investigate
        ↓
Document
        ↓
Validate
        ↓
Build
        ↓
Aggregate
        ↓
Publish
        ↓
Agent Update
        ↓
Continue Collection

Engineering Principle

STN-LABZ intelligence progresses through a defined engineering pipeline from external discovery to operational agent knowledge.

Each intelligence item maintains an identifiable path through that pipeline.

The INT identifier connects investigation and operator control.

Controlled-document identity connects intelligence to governed documentation.

The Trust Chain establishes the applicable validation evidence.

rag_builder transforms validated controlled information into corpus data.

Corpus aggregation produces the authoritative kb.corpus.json.

Agents validate and index that corpus as their operational knowledge source.

The complete engineering relationship is:

Intelligence
        ↓
Investigation
        ↓
Controlled Documentation
        ↓
Trust Chain
        ↓
Corpus Transformation
        ↓
Corpus Aggregation
        ↓
Agent Validation
        ↓
Operational Knowledge
