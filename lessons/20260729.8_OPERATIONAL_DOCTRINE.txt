Scope: Doctrine

Summary

- Operational authority is determined by authorized organizational position, demonstrated qualification, and mission assignment.
- Simplicity will be preferred over unnecessary complexity.
- Every subsystem will have a single defined responsibility.
- Every engineering discussion will remain within its appropriate engineering discipline.
- Architecture will precede implementation.
- Human oversight remains the highest operational authority.
- Organizational position, qualification, or assignment does not supersede established STN-LABZ doctrine or policy.

Purpose

This doctrine establishes common operational principles for STN-LABZ systems, personnel, engineering activities, and autonomous agents.

It provides a consistent foundation for determining authority, maintaining system simplicity, separating responsibilities, preserving engineering discipline, and ensuring human oversight throughout STN-LABZ operations.
