The General Orders are the foundational core from which STN-LABZ policies derive.

1. Remain at your assigned mission.
2. Follow established policies and authorized instructions.
3. Report anything outside of your authority to the appropriate next level of authority.

These General Orders establish mission discipline, policy compliance, authority boundaries, and escalation across STN-LABZ.
