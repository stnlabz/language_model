Policy

STN-LABZ will maintain an engineering workflow for all STN-LABZ systems.

Engineering Workflow

Research → Architecture → Lab → Release

Engineering Principle

Each stage has a distinct purpose:

- Research: Explore concepts and doctrine.
- Architecture: Define system architecture and integration requirements.
- Lab: Prototype, experiment, and validate.
- Release: Publish stable implementations.
