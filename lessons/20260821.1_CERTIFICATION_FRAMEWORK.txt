1. Purpose

This policy establishes the STN-LABZ Certification Framework governing certification as evidence of demonstrated competency for defined organizational positions, operational domains, systems, and mission functions.

Certification supports the STN-LABZ Tiered Position Framework by establishing whether an employee has demonstrated the competency required for consideration for particular positions and missions.

Certification does not itself constitute a mission assignment.

Certification does not itself provide operational access.

Within STN-LABZ:

> Mission Assignment = Access

Certification is therefore a prerequisite where required, not an access mechanism.

---

2. Scope

This policy applies to STN-LABZ certifications associated with:

- organizational positions;
- Position Tiers;
- Mission Control;
- Digit;
- Ezra;
- Rictus Intelligence;
- Sentinel;
- engineering functions;
- intelligence functions;
- cybersecurity functions;
- space operations;
- system-specific functions;
- mission-specific competencies; and
- future controlled operational domains requiring demonstrated competency.

Individual certifications will be defined by subordinate certification standards.

---

3. Certification Role

Certification establishes that an individual has demonstrated competency against a defined STN-LABZ certification standard.

Certification may establish eligibility for:

- assignment to a defined Position;
- advancement to a Position requiring the certification;
- qualification for specific mission functions;
- consideration for missions involving controlled operational systems; and
- specialized responsibilities defined by applicable STN-LABZ documentation.

Certification establishes competency.

Mission assignment establishes access.

The relationship is:

    EMPLOYMENT
        ↓
    POSITION
        ↓
    POSITION TIER
        ↓
    REQUIRED CERTIFICATION / QUALIFICATION
        ↓
    MISSION ASSIGNMENT
        =
    MISSION ACCESS

---

4. Certification and Position Tiers

Position Tiers establish organizational eligibility for consideration for particular responsibilities and controlled operational domains.

Certification establishes demonstrated competency required within that eligibility structure.

A certification may identify one or more Position Tiers for which it is applicable.

Holding a certification does not elevate an employee's Position Tier.

Holding a Position Tier does not imply possession of required certifications.

An employee considered for a Position or mission will satisfy both the applicable Position Tier requirements and required certification requirements.

---

5. Certification and Positions

Controlled STN-LABZ Positions may identify required certifications.

A Position definition may require:

- one general STN-LABZ certification;
- one or more domain-specific certifications;
- system-specific certifications;
- position-specific certifications; or
- additional qualification requirements established by the applicable Position definition.

Certification requirements will be defined by the Position or by the applicable certification standard.

An employee will not be considered qualified for a Position requiring a certification until the required certification is current.

---

6. Certification and Mission Assignment

Certification supports mission eligibility.

Certification does not create a mission assignment.

An employee may hold every certification required for a controlled operational domain and still have no access to that domain when no applicable mission is assigned.

For example:

    Employee Status: ACTIVE
    Position: QUALIFIED
    Position Tier: ELIGIBLE
    Required Certification: CURRENT
    Mission Assignment: NONE

    Operational Access: NONE

When an applicable mission is assigned:

    Employee Status: ACTIVE
    Position: QUALIFIED
    Position Tier: ELIGIBLE
    Required Certification: CURRENT
    Mission Assignment: ACTIVE

    Mission Access: ACTIVE

When the mission ends, mission access ends.

The certification remains a record of demonstrated competency unless its own lifecycle status changes.

---

7. Certification Categories

STN-LABZ certifications may be organized into certification categories according to organizational need.

Initial certification domains include:

- STN-LABZ General Engineering;
- Mission Control;
- Digit Engineering;
- Ezra Space Operations;
- Rictus Intelligence;
- Sentinel Security Operations.

Additional certifications may be established when a defined Position, system, operational domain, or mission function requires a distinct demonstrated competency.

Certification categories will not be created solely for organizational decoration.

Each certification will correspond to a defined competency requirement.

---

8. General Certifications

A General Certification establishes competency applicable across multiple STN-LABZ Positions or operational domains.

A General Certification may establish foundational competency in areas including:

- STN-LABZ engineering practices;
- documentation requirements;
- development doctrine;
- controlled-system architecture;
- qualification processes;
- policy application; and
- organizational operational doctrine.

General Certifications may be prerequisites for specialized certifications where established by the applicable certification standard.

---

9. Domain-Specific Certifications

A Domain-Specific Certification establishes competency within a defined STN-LABZ operational domain.

Domain-specific certification areas may include:

9.1 Mission Control

Certification applicable to Mission Control responsibilities and mission-management functions.

9.2 Digit Engineering

Certification applicable to engineering work involving Digit and its approved engineering functions.

9.3 Ezra Space Operations

Certification applicable to space operations and Ezra-related mission functions.

9.4 Rictus Intelligence

Certification applicable to intelligence and communications functions associated with Rictus Intelligence.

9.5 Sentinel Security Operations

Certification applicable to cybersecurity and defensive operations associated with Sentinel.

Each domain-specific certification will be governed by its own certification standard.

---

10. Certification Standard Requirements

Every STN-LABZ certification will have a defined certification standard.

Each certification standard will identify at minimum:

- Certification ID
- Certification Name
- Status
- Purpose
- Applicable Position Tiers
- Applicable Positions
- Operational Domain
- Prerequisites
- Required Knowledge
- Required Competencies
- Required Practical Demonstration
- Qualification Requirements
- Evidence Requirements
- Evaluation Method
- Passing Requirements
- Certification Authority
- Certification Lifecycle
- References

The certification standard establishes what must be demonstrated.

The certification record establishes whether a particular individual successfully demonstrated it.

---

11. Evidence

Certification decisions will be supported by evidence demonstrating that the certification requirements were satisfied.

Evidence may include:

- completed engineering work;
- qualification results;
- practical demonstrations;
- test results;
- technical review;
- documented project work;
- controlled documentation;
- code or system artifacts;
- mission simulations;
- operational exercises;
- examination results; and
- other evidence defined by the applicable certification standard.

The required evidence will be established by the certification standard.

Evidence requirements will correspond to the competency being certified.

---

12. Practical Competency

Where practical competency is part of the certification requirement, the certification process will require demonstrated performance rather than knowledge claims alone.

Examples may include:

- building a compliant component;
- diagnosing a defined engineering condition;
- completing a qualification sequence;
- reviewing controlled engineering evidence;
- operating within a defined mission simulation;
- demonstrating system-specific procedures; or
- completing another practical requirement defined by the certification standard.

Certification will reflect demonstrated competency against the applicable standard.

---

13. Certification Evaluation

Certification evaluation will compare the candidate's evidence against the requirements of the applicable certification standard.

Evaluation results will identify whether the candidate:

- satisfies the certification requirements;
- requires additional work;
- does not satisfy the certification requirements; or
- must repeat a defined qualification or practical requirement.

The certification standard may establish additional evaluation states where needed.

---

14. Certification Records

Each issued certification will have a certification record.

A certification record will identify at minimum:

- Credential ID
- Certification ID
- Certification Name
- Certification Standard Revision
- Employee or Candidate Identifier
- Position, when applicable
- Position Tier, when applicable
- Status
- Date Awarded
- Expiration Date, when applicable
- Evidence References
- Reviewer or Certifying Authority
- Decision Date

Certification records will preserve the basis upon which the certification was awarded.

---

15. Credential Identification

Each issued STN-LABZ certification will receive a unique credential identifier.

The credential identifier will uniquely identify the certification record.

Credential identifiers will not be reused.

The credential record will remain associated with the certification and certification standard against which the individual was evaluated.

---

16. Certification Lifecycle

STN-LABZ certifications may use the following lifecycle states:

- Pending
- Active
- Suspended
- Expired
- Revoked

16.1 Pending

The certification process has not yet resulted in an active certification.

16.2 Active

The certification requirements have been satisfied and the certification is current.

16.3 Suspended

The certification temporarily does not satisfy current certification status requirements.

16.4 Expired

The certification has reached an established expiration condition.

16.5 Revoked

The certification has been withdrawn according to the applicable certification requirements or organizational action.

The lifecycle of a certification does not alter the historical record showing that the certification was previously awarded.

---

17. Certification Validity

A certification required by a Position or mission will satisfy that requirement only while the certification is in a valid status defined by the applicable standard.

Unless otherwise defined by the applicable certification standard, Active is the status representing a current certification.

Suspended, Expired, or Revoked certifications do not satisfy a requirement for a current certification.

---

18. Expiration and Renewal

A certification may be permanent or time-limited according to the applicable certification standard.

Where expiration applies, the certification standard will identify:

- certification validity period;
- expiration conditions;
- renewal requirements;
- reevaluation requirements; and
- required evidence for renewal.

A certification without an established expiration requirement will not be assigned an arbitrary expiration period.

---

19. Suspension

A certification may be suspended where an applicable organizational or certification requirement establishes a basis for suspension.

Suspension prevents the certification from satisfying a requirement for current certification while the suspension remains in effect.

The certification record will remain intact.

Restoration from suspension will follow the applicable certification requirements.

---

20. Revocation

A certification may be revoked where an applicable organizational or certification requirement establishes a basis for revocation.

Revocation changes the certification status.

It does not delete the certification record or its historical evidence.

A revoked certification will not satisfy certification requirements for a Position or mission.

---

21. Certification Standard Revisions

A certification will identify the revision of the certification standard against which the individual was evaluated.

A later revision of a certification standard does not silently alter the historical basis of an existing certification.

Where a new certification-standard revision introduces requirements affecting existing certifications, the revised standard or another applicable controlled document will define the effect on those certifications.

Existing certification history will remain traceable to the standard under which certification occurred.

---

22. Qualification Relationship

Certification and qualification are related but distinct concepts.

Certification records demonstrated competency against a certification standard.

Qualification may include:

- certification;
- system-specific qualification;
- practical qualification;
- mission-specific qualification;
- recency requirements; and
- other competency requirements established by the applicable Position or mission.

A Position or mission may require both certification and additional qualification.

Certification does not automatically imply satisfaction of every qualification requirement.

---

23. Mission Control Certification

Mission Control certification standards will define competencies required for positions responsible for mission planning, assignment, coordination, direction, reporting, and associated Mission Control functions.

Mission Control certification does not create a Mission Control mission assignment.

Mission Control access exists when the employee is assigned to the applicable Mission Control mission or function.

28. Certification and Mission Access

Certification will not be used as a substitute for mission assignment.

The governing relationship is:

    POSITION TIER
        ↓
    CERTIFICATION / QUALIFICATION
        ↓
    ELIGIBLE FOR MISSION ASSIGNMENT
        ↓
    MISSION ASSIGNMENT
        =
    ACCESS TO THE ASSIGNED MISSION

Accordingly:

> Certification proves competency.

> Mission assignment grants access.

> No mission means no mission access.

---

29. Certification Framework Principle

The STN-LABZ Certification Framework exists to establish evidence of competency required for organizational positions and missions.

Certification is not a general access credential.

Certification is not a substitute for Position Tier.

Certification is not a mission assignment.

Certification provides evidence that an employee has satisfied defined competency requirements and may therefore be considered for positions and missions requiring that certification.
