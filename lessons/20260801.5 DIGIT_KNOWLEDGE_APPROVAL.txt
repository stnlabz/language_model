Policy

All information intended for Digit's operational use will be reviewed and approved by qualified STN-LABZ engineers before incorporation into an authorized STN-LABZ knowledge source.

Intelligence and operational data derived from Rictus or STN-LABZ research will require human approval through the applicable Digit and Rictus project authority before becoming authorized knowledge for Digit.

Qualified engineers will verify, as applicable:

- Accuracy
- Relevance
- Authenticity
- Mission applicability

Unapproved information will not be made available to Digit as operational knowledge through a knowledge base, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system, intranet resource, or other authorized knowledge mechanism.

Information approved for Digit's operational use will be treated as authorized STN-LABZ engineering knowledge within the scope for which it was approved.

Approval does not grant Digit authority to independently acquire, approve, expand, or modify her authoritative knowledge sources.

Rationale

This policy ensures that Digit's engineering knowledge remains accurate, auditable, deterministic, mission-relevant, and under qualified human control throughout the STN-LABZ ecosystem.
