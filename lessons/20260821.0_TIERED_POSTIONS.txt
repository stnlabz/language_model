1. Purpose

This policy establishes the STN-LABZ Tiered Position Framework governing organizational positions, position tiers, qualification eligibility, mission assignment, and access to controlled operational systems.

STN-LABZ will separate employment, organizational position, qualification, mission assignment, and operational access into distinct authority boundaries.

The framework is based on the principle:

> Employment ≠ Position ≠ Qualification ≠ Mission Assignment ≠ Access

No individual receives operational access solely because they are employed by STN-LABZ, occupy a particular position, hold a certification, or have previously performed work within an operational domain.

Operational access exists only when all required authority conditions are simultaneously satisfied.

---

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- STN-LABZ employees;
- contractors where explicitly authorized;
- controlled organizational positions;
- certification and qualification programs;
- Mission Control;
- Digit;
- Ezra;
- Rictus Intelligence;
- Sentinel;
- future STN-LABZ controlled operational systems;
- mission-specific information;
- mission-specific tools;
- mission-specific infrastructure; and
- access authorization associated with controlled operations.

This policy governs eligibility and access relationships.

Individual position requirements, certification requirements, system qualification requirements, and mission requirements may be defined by subordinate controlled documents.

---

3. Organizational Authority Model

STN-LABZ will maintain the following authority progression:

    EMPLOYMENT
        ↓
    POSITION
        ↓
    POSITION TIER
        ↓
    SYSTEM ELIGIBILITY
        ↓
    CERTIFICATION / QUALIFICATION
        ↓
    MISSION ASSIGNMENT
        ↓
    MISSION-SPECIFIC ACCESS
        ↓
    AUTHORIZED OPERATION

Each stage is independently required where applicable.

Passing one stage does not imply satisfaction of any subsequent stage.

---

4. Employment

Employment establishes an individual's organizational relationship with STN-LABZ.

Employment alone does not:

- establish qualification;
- grant certification;
- establish mission authority;
- grant controlled-system access;
- authorize access to mission information;
- authorize operational actions; or
- establish eligibility for every STN-LABZ system.

An active employee with no authorized mission assignment may possess no operational access.

---

5. Positions

A Position is a defined organizational role within STN-LABZ.

An employee may be hired, appointed, or transferred into a Position according to applicable organizational requirements.

Each controlled Position will define its organizational responsibilities and its Position Tier.

Position assignment establishes organizational placement.

It does not independently grant operational access.

---

6. Position Tiers

Every controlled STN-LABZ Position will be assigned a Position Tier.

Position Tier establishes the maximum class of operational responsibility for which the Position may be considered.

Position Tier may determine eligibility for:

- controlled systems;
- sensitive operational functions;
- specialized qualifications;
- mission assignments;
- command responsibilities;
- intelligence functions;
- security functions;
- engineering functions; and
- space operations.

A Position Tier establishes eligibility for consideration.

It does not establish authorization.

> Position Tier determines eligibility for consideration. It does not grant access.

---

7. System Eligibility

Controlled STN-LABZ systems and operational domains may establish a minimum Position Tier.

An employee whose Position Tier is below the required tier is not eligible for mission assignment granting access to that system.

Meeting or exceeding the minimum Position Tier does not automatically grant access.

Eligibility only permits the employee to proceed to applicable certification, qualification, mission-assignment, and authorization requirements.

---

8. Qualification and Certification

Qualification establishes demonstrated competency to perform a defined function.

Certification may form part of qualification where required by the applicable Position, system, mission, or operational function.

Qualification requirements may include:

- technical competency;
- certification;
- system-specific training;
- operational procedures;
- policy knowledge;
- security requirements;
- demonstrated proficiency;
- successful practical evaluation;
- required qualification testing; and
- other documented competency requirements.

Qualification does not establish operational need.

Qualification therefore does not independently grant access.

> Qualification demonstrates competency. It does not grant access.

---

9. Mission Assignment

Mission assignment establishes the operational need for an employee to perform defined work.

Mission assignment is a required authority boundary for controlled operational access.

An employee will not receive operational access merely because the employee:

- works for STN-LABZ;
- holds a sufficiently high Position Tier;
- possesses the required certifications;
- has completed qualification;
- previously held access; or
- previously participated in a related mission.

An active mission assignment must exist.

> No active mission assignment means no mission access.

---

10. Mission-Specific Access

Access granted pursuant to a mission assignment will be limited to the systems, information, functions, tools, and resources required by that mission.

Mission access will not automatically extend to unrelated systems or missions.

An employee authorized for one mission may remain unauthorized for another mission involving the same system.

An employee holding a high-tier Position will not receive general access to all lower-tier operational domains.

Access remains mission-specific.

---

11. Access Authorization Requirements

Operational access to a controlled STN-LABZ system requires all applicable conditions to be satisfied.

At minimum:

1. the employee status is active;
2. the employee occupies an active Position;
3. the Position Tier satisfies the minimum system eligibility requirement;
4. all required certifications are valid;
5. all required qualifications are current;
6. an active mission assignment exists;
7. the mission assignment requires the requested system or resource;
8. the requested access falls within the employee's assigned mission authority; and
9. no suspension, restriction, or other controlling condition prohibits access.

Failure of any required condition results in denial.

---

12. Fail-Closed Access Decision

STN-LABZ operational access will be evaluated using a fail-closed model.

    IF employee status is not active
        DENY

    IF position status is not active
        DENY

    IF position tier does not satisfy system eligibility
        DENY

    IF required certification is missing or invalid
        DENY

    IF required qualification is missing or invalid
        DENY

    IF active mission assignment does not exist
        DENY

    IF requested system is outside mission authority
        DENY

    IF requested resource is outside mission scope
        DENY

    ALLOW only the access explicitly authorized by the active mission

Missing, conflicting, ambiguous, or unverifiable authority information will not be interpreted as authorization.

Access will remain denied until authority is established.

13. Controlled Operational Domains

13.1 Mission Control

Mission Control is the human command and operational authority domain of STN-LABZ.

Mission Control positions may be authorized to perform functions including:

- mission assignment;
- operational direction;
- mission approval;
- access authorization;
- qualification review;
- escalation handling;
- mission suspension;
- mission termination; and
- release of operational authority.

Mission Control authority will remain bounded by the Position, Position Tier, assigned responsibilities, and applicable policy.

Mission Control does not imply unrestricted access to every operational system.

13.2 Digit

Digit is a controlled STN-LABZ engineering intelligence operational domain.

Eligibility for Digit access will require an applicable Position Tier.

Operational Digit access additionally requires:

- applicable certifications;
- applicable engineering or operational qualification;
- an active Digit-related mission assignment; and
- mission authority requiring Digit access.

An employee qualified to work with Digit but not assigned to a Digit mission will have no Digit operational access.

13.3 Ezra

Ezra is a controlled STN-LABZ space operations domain.

Eligibility for Ezra access will require an applicable Position Tier.

Operational Ezra access additionally requires:

- applicable space-systems certification;
- applicable technical qualification;
- mission-specific qualification where required;
- an active Ezra or space mission assignment; and
- mission authority requiring Ezra access.

Ezra access will remain limited to the assigned mission.

13.4 Rictus Intelligence

Rictus Intelligence is a controlled STN-LABZ intelligence and communications operational domain.

Eligibility for Rictus Intelligence access will require an applicable Position Tier.

Operational Rictus Intelligence access additionally requires:

- applicable intelligence or communications qualification;
- applicable certifications;
- an active Rictus-related mission assignment; and
- mission authority requiring access.

Access to Rictus Intelligence does not independently authorize access to Digit, Ezra, Sentinel, or unrelated intelligence missions.

13.5 Sentinel

Sentinel is a controlled STN-LABZ cybersecurity and defensive operations domain.

Eligibility for Sentinel access will require an applicable Position Tier.

Operational Sentinel access additionally requires:

- applicable security certification;
- applicable defensive-operations qualification;
- an active Sentinel-related mission assignment; and
- mission authority requiring Sentinel access.

Sentinel access will remain bounded to the assigned defensive mission and authorized systems.

---

14. Multiple Positions

An employee may occupy multiple authorized Positions where organizational requirements permit.

Each Position retains its own:

- Position Tier;
- qualification requirements;
- certification requirements;
- system eligibility;
- mission eligibility; and
- authority boundaries.

Holding multiple Positions does not combine their authorities into unrestricted access.

Access will be evaluated according to the Position and mission under which the employee is currently operating.

---

15. Position Definition Requirements

Every controlled STN-LABZ Position will define at minimum:

- Position ID
- Position Name
- Position Tier
- Position Type
- Operational Domain
- Reports To
- Position Purpose
- Responsibilities
- Required Certifications
- Required Qualifications
- Eligible Systems
- Mission Eligibility
- Mission Authority
- Access Limitations
- Escalation Authority
- Status

Additional requirements may be established where necessary.

---

16. Position Tier and System Mapping

Position tiers and controlled-system eligibility will be defined in a subordinate controlled document.

That document will establish the Position Tier structure and identify the minimum tier eligible for consideration for controlled domains including:

- Mission Control;
- Digit;
- Ezra;
- Rictus Intelligence; and
- Sentinel.

System eligibility tables will not themselves grant access.

---

17. Access Removal

Operational access will be removed when any required authorization condition ceases to exist.

Conditions requiring access removal include:

- mission completion;
- mission cancellation;
- mission reassignment;
- withdrawal of mission authority;
- employment suspension;
- employment termination;
- Position suspension;
- Position reassignment;
- certification suspension;
- certification expiration;
- certification revocation;
- qualification expiration;
- qualification withdrawal; or
- loss of any other required authorization condition.

Access removal does not alter historical records documenting previously authorized activity.

---

18. Historical Authority

Previous authorization does not establish current authorization.

Prior mission assignment, prior system access, prior qualification, or prior operational responsibility will not be used as evidence of current access authority.

Historical records will remain available for audit and organizational history according to applicable STN-LABZ record-control requirements.

---

19. Separation of Eligibility and Authority

STN-LABZ will maintain an explicit distinction between eligibility and authority.

Position Tier

Defines whether an employee may be considered for a controlled operational responsibility.

Qualification

Demonstrates whether the employee is competent to perform the required function.

Mission Assignment

Establishes whether STN-LABZ currently requires the employee to perform that function.

Access Authorization

Defines the exact resources required to execute the authorized mission.

These conditions are cumulative.

None substitutes for another.

---

20. General Orders Alignment

This framework supports the STN-LABZ General Orders:

1. Remain at your assigned mission.
2. Follow established policies and authorized instructions.
3. Report anything outside your authority to the appropriate next level of authority.

Mission assignment establishes the operational boundary within which authorized activity occurs.

Requests or conditions outside that mission boundary will be treated as outside existing authority unless separately authorized.

---

21. Authority Principle

STN-LABZ will grant controlled operational access through explicit authority rather than organizational assumption.

The governing principles of this framework are:

> Employment does not grant access.

> Position does not grant access.

> Position Tier determines eligibility for consideration. It does not grant access.

> Qualification demonstrates competency. It does not grant access.

> Mission assignment establishes operational need.

> Access exists only where eligibility, qualification, mission assignment, and authorization agree.
