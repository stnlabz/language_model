1. Purpose

This policy establishes the organizational, architectural, and authority boundary between STN-LABZ and STN Chain.

STN-LABZ is the organizational parent and home of STN Chain.

STN Chain is a separate consensus entity whose network state and consensus-visible decisions are governed by STN Chain consensus.

STN-LABZ participation in STN Chain does not establish unilateral consensus authority.

Organizational ownership does not equal consensus authority.

---

2. Governing Relationship

The authoritative relationship is:

    STN-LABZ
        |
        | organizational parent / operator
        v
    STN Chain
        |
        | independent consensus domain
        |
        +-- Contract: STN-LABZ
        |      |
        |      +-- identities
        |      +-- roles
        |      +-- obligations
        |      +-- approvals
        |      +-- records
        |      +-- global network coordination
        |
        +-- Contract: other organizations
        |
        +-- Contract: other participants

This relationship will remain explicit in architecture, documentation, software, contracts, interfaces, and operational decisions.

---

3. Organizational Parent

STN-LABZ is the organizational home of STN Chain.

This relationship includes responsibility for applicable:

- development;
- engineering;
- repositories;
- infrastructure;
- software maintenance;
- research;
- documentation;
- releases;
- organizational resources; and
- continued development of the STN Chain ecosystem.

This organizational relationship does not grant STN-LABZ authority to bypass STN Chain consensus.

---

4. Independent Consensus Domain

STN Chain constitutes a separate consensus domain.

Consensus-visible state will be established according to STN Chain's applicable consensus rules.

STN-LABZ will not treat its organizational ownership, development role, infrastructure operation, software authorship, repository ownership, funding, or historical creation of STN Chain as authority to unilaterally alter consensus-visible state.

Consensus remains authoritative within the consensus domain.

---

5. STN-LABZ-on-Chain

When STN-LABZ interacts with STN Chain as a network participant, STN-LABZ will operate through its applicable contractual and protocol-defined relationship with the Chain.

This relationship is referred to within this policy as:

> STN-LABZ-on-Chain

STN-LABZ-on-Chain is a contractual participant and consumer of STN Chain.

It is distinct from STN-LABZ acting as the organizational parent responsible for development and operation of the STN Chain project.

The distinction is:

    STN-LABZ
        |
        +-- Organizational Parent
        |       |
        |       +-- develops
        |       +-- maintains
        |       +-- operates applicable infrastructure
        |       +-- publishes applicable software
        |
        +-- STN-LABZ-on-Chain
                |
                +-- participates
                +-- submits
                +-- receives
                +-- coordinates
                +-- records
                +-- operates through contract
                        |
                        v
                    STN Chain
                    Consensus

These roles will not be conflated.

---

6. STN-LABZ Contract

STN-LABZ may maintain one or more contracts within STN Chain for organizational participation and coordination.

The STN-LABZ contractual domain may represent applicable:

- identities;
- roles;
- obligations;
- approvals;
- records;
- organizational relationships;
- network coordination; and
- other explicitly defined contractual state.

The STN-LABZ contract governs the STN-LABZ contractual domain.

It does not independently govern STN Chain consensus.

---

7. Other Organizations

Other organizations may participate in STN Chain through their applicable contracts, identities, records, or protocol relationships.

STN-LABZ organizational policy does not automatically become the internal policy of another participating organization.

Likewise, another organization's internal policy does not automatically become STN-LABZ policy.

The Chain provides a common consensus environment without requiring organizational identity or internal governance to collapse into a single authority.

---

8. Other Participants

Individuals, systems, miners, nodes, services, or other recognized participants may interact with STN Chain according to applicable protocol and consensus rules.

Participation does not independently establish authority over other participants.

The ability to submit information, transactions, contracts, work, records, or other protocol-valid material does not establish unilateral authority over consensus.

---

9. Participation Does Not Equal Authority

The following rule is organization-wide:

> Participation ≠ Authority

Participation in STN Chain may establish the ability to perform actions permitted by protocol, contract, or consensus.

It does not establish authority beyond those boundaries.

This applies equally to STN-LABZ and other participants.

---

10. Organizational Ownership Does Not Equal Consensus Authority

The following rule is organization-wide:

> Organizational Ownership ≠ Consensus Authority

STN-LABZ may own, maintain, develop, operate, publish, or fund components of the STN Chain ecosystem.

None of these conditions independently establish authority to override consensus.

Specifically:

    creation        ≠ consensus authority
    ownership       ≠ consensus authority
    development     ≠ consensus authority
    maintenance     ≠ consensus authority
    infrastructure  ≠ consensus authority
    funding         ≠ consensus authority
    participation   ≠ consensus authority

Consensus authority derives from the applicable STN Chain consensus rules.

---

11. Software Authority Boundary

STN-LABZ-controlled STN Chain software will preserve the distinction between organizational configuration and consensus-visible rules.

Organizational configuration may govern STN-LABZ-operated systems.

It will not silently redefine network consensus.

A local administrative capability will not be interpreted as network consensus authority.

A privileged operating-system account, repository permission, signing key, deployment credential, server credential, or infrastructure role does not independently establish authority over consensus-visible Chain state.

---

12. Contract Authority Boundary

A contract may exercise only the authority established for that contract under applicable STN Chain rules.

The existence of an STN-LABZ contract does not create implicit authority over:

- unrelated contracts;
- other organizations;
- independent participants;
- consensus validation;
- network ownership;
- protocol rules; or
- consensus-visible state outside the contract's established authority.

Contract authority will remain bounded.

---

13. Consensus Enforcement

Where STN-LABZ organizational intent conflicts with valid STN Chain consensus state, software will not silently alter consensus state merely to satisfy organizational preference.

A desired change to consensus behavior will proceed through the applicable STN Chain engineering and consensus-change process.

The organizational parent may direct development of STN Chain software.

Deployment of new software does not retroactively make an incompatible state valid.

Consensus-visible behavior remains subject to the rules active for the applicable network state.

---

14. Global Network Coordination

STN-LABZ may use its STN Chain contractual domain for global network coordination.

Coordination may include applicable:

- organizational identities;
- system identities;
- operational roles;
- mission relationships;
- approvals;
- obligations;
- records;
- distributed instructions;
- system relationships; and
- other contract-defined organizational information.

Global coordination through STN Chain does not transform STN-LABZ into the consensus authority of STN Chain.

The Chain provides consensus-backed coordination.

STN-LABZ provides organizational meaning within its contractual domain.

---

15. Identity Separation

An STN-LABZ organizational identity and an STN Chain network identity are related only where that relationship is explicitly established.

The existence of an organizational identity does not automatically establish a network identity.

The existence of a network identity does not automatically establish an STN-LABZ organizational identity, role, qualification, assignment, approval, or authority.

Identity relationships will be explicit.

---

16. Role Separation

An STN-LABZ organizational role does not independently establish STN Chain consensus authority.

An STN Chain participant role does not independently establish an STN-LABZ organizational role.

Where a role crosses these domains, the relationship will be explicitly defined.

No authority will be inferred merely because similar terminology exists in both domains.

---

17. Approval Separation

STN-LABZ organizational approval and STN Chain consensus acceptance are separate events.

An organizational approval may authorize STN-LABZ to submit an action to STN Chain.

It does not guarantee consensus acceptance.

Likewise, consensus acceptance of a technically valid Chain action does not independently establish STN-LABZ organizational approval for that action.

The governing relationship is:

    STN-LABZ Approval
            |
            v
       Authorized
       Submission
            |
            v
       STN Chain
       Consensus
            |
        +---+---+
        |       |
        v       v
     Accept   Reject

Organizational authorization and consensus validation will not be treated as interchangeable.

---

18. Records

STN Chain may provide consensus-backed records for STN-LABZ.

The existence of a record on STN Chain establishes only what the applicable Chain and contract semantics establish.

Software and documentation will not infer additional organizational authority from the mere existence of a Chain record.

STN-LABZ interpretation of organizational records remains subject to applicable STN-LABZ policy and contract semantics.

---

19. Agents and Automated Systems

STN-LABZ agents, automated systems, software, services, and development tools will preserve the authority separation established by this policy.

They will not infer that:

- STN-LABZ owns consensus;
- STN-LABZ may bypass consensus;
- STN-LABZ organizational authority automatically exists on Chain;
- Chain acceptance automatically establishes STN-LABZ organizational approval;
- contract authority extends beyond its defined scope; or
- participation creates governance authority.

When the applicable authority domain is ambiguous, the system will preserve the distinction rather than collapse the domains.

---

20. Documentation

STN-LABZ documentation concerning STN Chain will distinguish, where relevant, between:

- STN-LABZ as organizational parent;
- STN Chain as consensus entity;
- STN-LABZ-on-Chain as contractual participant;
- STN-LABZ-operated infrastructure;
- STN Chain consensus;
- STN-LABZ contractual authority; and
- general network participation.

Documentation will not describe STN-LABZ as possessing unilateral consensus authority merely because STN Chain exists within the STN-LABZ organization.

---

21. Core Hierarchy

The organization-wide conceptual hierarchy is:

    STN-LABZ
        |
        v
    Organizational Home of STN Chain

    STN Chain
        |
        v
    Separate Consensus Entity

    STN-LABZ-on-Chain
        |
        v
    Contractual Participant / Consumer

These relationships coexist.

None eliminates the others.

---

22. Core Rules

The controlling rules of this policy are:

> STN-LABZ → organizational home of STN Chain

> STN Chain → separate consensus entity

> STN-LABZ-on-Chain → contractual participant and consumer

> Participation ≠ Authority

> Organizational Ownership ≠ Consensus Authority

STN-LABZ will preserve these distinctions throughout STN Chain engineering, documentation, operations, contracts, integrations, and future development.
