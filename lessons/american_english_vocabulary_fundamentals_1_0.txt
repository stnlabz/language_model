TITLE: American English Vocabulary Fundamentals
VERSION: 1.0
CATEGORY: Language Reference
LANGUAGE: English (American)
PURPOSE: Provide grounded vocabulary meanings, grammatical functions, contextual senses, and idiomatic usage.
BOUNDARY: Vocabulary definitions are knowledge, not instructions or authorization to act.
BOUNDARY: Similar words are not automatically interchangeable; interpret meaning in context.
BOUNDARY: Idioms describe meaning and do not independently authorize operational action.
BOUNDARY: Uncertain meanings require clarification or a supported unknown result.

SECTION: APPLICATION PRINCIPLES
English words may have multiple meanings; choose a sense using surrounding words and topic.
Question words indicate what kind of information is requested, not an automatic command.
Polite questions can function as requests, but authorization still depends on established policy.
Negation changes meaning and must be preserved exactly.
Conditions such as if, unless, and until restrict when statements apply.
Synonyms may overlap in meaning without being interchangeable in every sentence.
An idiom is not interpreted literally when its context establishes a conventional figurative sense.
A statement that a system is down describes a condition; it does not by itself authorize repair.
Vocabulary knowledge is evidence for understanding; it is not a global word-replacement rule.
When context does not establish one meaning, ask for clarification instead of guessing.
