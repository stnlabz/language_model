Doctrine

Within STN-LABZ, unknown situations will never be guessed by any autonomous or non-autonomous system.

Process

- Known Safe → Allow
- Known Unsafe → Block
- Known Recoverable → Recover
- Unknown → Preserve → Contain → Escalate to Human

Engineering Principle

This establishes human judgment as the final authority for uncertain situations.
