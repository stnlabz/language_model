Doctrine

STN-LABZ engineering will progress through a continuing evidence-driven development cycle.

The governing engineering progression is:

Requirement / Engineering Need
        ↓
Define Expected Behavior
        ↓
Engineer Implementation
        ↓
Compile / Build
        ↓
Verify Implementation Works
        ↓
Establish Applicable Qualification Test(s)
        ↓
Run Qualification
        ↓
Failure → Investigate → Fix → Regression Test
        ↓
Continue Development
        ↓
Full Applicable Bench
        ↓
ALL PASS / NO OPEN REGRESSIONS
        ↓
QUALIFIED
        ↓
Integration
        ↓
Release / Operational Evaluation
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Next Development Cycle


This is how engineering work progresses through STN-LABZ.

Applicable established policies govern specialized requirements encountered during that progression.

The development cycle does not replace applicable policy, qualification requirements, project requirements, or human authority.

Evidence produced during development, qualification, integration, release, and operational evaluation informs subsequent development cycles.

Development Cycle

Requirement / Engineering Need

Engineering begins with an identified requirement or demonstrated engineering need.

The requirement establishes what capability, correction, change, or engineering outcome is required.

Requirements may originate from engineering decisions, research, operational evidence, defects, incidents, security findings, qualification findings, maintenance requirements, or other authorized sources.

Define Expected Behavior

Expected behavior will be defined before qualification.

The expected behavior provides the basis against which the resulting implementation can be evaluated and qualification requirements established.

Unknown or unresolved behavior will not be silently converted into an assumed requirement.

Engineer Implementation

The implementation will be developed according to applicable architecture, engineering requirements, standards, policies, and authorized project decisions.

Implementation convenience does not override established requirements or policy.

Compile / Build

The implementation will successfully compile or otherwise pass the applicable project build-validation boundary before qualification begins.

Successful compilation or build validation establishes only that the implementation has passed the applicable build-validation boundary.

It does not constitute qualification.

Verify Implementation Works

The implementation will be evaluated during development to determine whether it performs the expected behavior before formal qualification.

Development verification demonstrates implementation behavior.

Ordinary successful operation does not establish qualification.

Successful execution under expected conditions does not demonstrate behavior under applicable failure, boundary, recovery, malformed-input, security, or other negative conditions.

Development verification does not replace qualification.

Establish Applicable Qualification Tests

Qualification tests will be established from applicable:

- Requirements.
- Expected behavior.
- Architecture.
- Interfaces.
- Operational boundaries.
- Identified risks.
- Failure conditions.
- Security requirements.
- Recovery requirements.
- Engineering evidence.
- Previous defects and regressions.

Established minimum qualification test counts are qualification floors and do not establish maximum test counts.

Qualification suites may expand when engineering evidence establishes additional conditions requiring verification.

Applicable negative validation will be included according to established qualification requirements.

Run Qualification

The applicable qualification suite will be executed against the implementation being qualified.

Qualification remains incomplete while any applicable required test is:

- FAIL
- NOT RUN
- Otherwise unresolved

A previously successful test does not establish qualification of behavior subsequently affected by implementation changes.

Qualification applies to the specific implementation revision demonstrated by the resulting qualification evidence.

Qualification Findings

A qualification failure is an engineering finding.

Qualification failures will be investigated rather than waived, suppressed, reclassified, or ignored merely to obtain a passing qualification result.

When qualification exposes a defect, missing boundary, incomplete behavior, regression, or other engineering condition requiring correction, the affected implementation returns to development.

Qualification is therefore an engineering activity and not merely a ceremonial release gate.

Failure / Regression

A qualification failure or identified regression will return the affected implementation to development.

The engineering progression is:

Failure
    ↓
Investigate
    ↓
Identify Cause
    ↓
Correct Implementation
    ↓
Regression Test
    ↓
Resume Applicable Qualification


A change intended to satisfy one requirement may affect behavior previously demonstrated by another test.

Previous PASS results will not be treated as permanently inherited after modification of the affected implementation.

All identified regressions will be corrected before qualification is established or restored.

Continue Development

Corrective development will continue until identified failures and regressions have been addressed and the implementation is ready for the full applicable qualification bench.

Development performed in response to qualification findings remains part of the same engineering evidence chain.

Full Applicable Bench

The complete applicable qualification suite will be executed against the implementation revision for which qualification is being determined.

Previously established qualification tests remain applicable while they continue to verify current requirements.

New qualification tests may be added when engineering requirements, defects, operational experience, security findings, architectural changes, or other evidence establish a need for additional verification.

The authoritative qualification result will represent the applicable qualification suite after development changes and regression corrections have been incorporated.

Qualification Boundary

Qualification requires:

ALL APPLICABLE TESTS PASS
NO REQUIRED TESTS NOT RUN
NO OPEN REGRESSIONS


An implementation is not qualified merely because a minimum number of tests have been executed.

An implementation is not qualified merely because it compiles.

An implementation is not qualified merely because it operates successfully under ordinary conditions.

All applicable qualification requirements and required tests will be successfully satisfied before qualification is established.

Ambiguous qualification results do not become PASS.

Qualification Evidence

Qualification will produce sufficient engineering evidence to establish what implementation was tested and what result was demonstrated.

Applicable qualification evidence will identify, as appropriate:

- Project.
- Component.
- Implementation revision or version.
- Applicable build or source identity.
- Qualification test.
- Expected result.
- Actual result.
- Qualification result.
- Execution environment.
- Timestamp.
- Relevant diagnostic evidence.

Qualification evidence will be preserved according to applicable STN-LABZ engineering documentation requirements.

Qualification attaches to the demonstrated implementation revision.

It is not automatically inherited by future modified implementations.

Integration

Qualified implementations may proceed to applicable system integration.

Qualification of an individual component does not automatically establish qualification of a larger integrated system.

Integration remains subject to applicable project requirements, policies, validation, qualification requirements, and engineering authority.

Release / Operational Evaluation

Integrated systems may proceed through applicable release and operational evaluation processes.

Release does not terminate engineering development.

Operational use may produce evidence unavailable during development or qualification.

That evidence remains part of the engineering process.

Evidence

Development, qualification, integration, release, and operational evaluation will produce engineering evidence.

Evidence may identify:

- New requirements.
- Defects.
- Regressions.
- Failure conditions.
- Security findings.
- Architectural concerns.
- Operational requirements.
- Recovery requirements.
- Boundary conditions.
- Maintenance requirements.
- Opportunities for improvement.

Applicable evidence will inform subsequent engineering decisions.

Evidence that contradicts an existing engineering assumption will not be discarded merely because the existing implementation previously passed qualification.

Next Development Cycle

Operational evidence, new requirements, research, incidents, defects, security findings, qualification findings, or engineering decisions may initiate another development cycle.

Completion of one development cycle does not terminate development of the project.

A qualified implementation may therefore return to development when evidence establishes a legitimate engineering need.

Requalification

Modification of a qualified implementation requires applicable requalification according to established policy.

Previous qualification is not inherited by a modified implementation.

The modified implementation will not be represented as qualified solely because an earlier revision was qualified.

Regression validation will determine whether previously demonstrated behavior remains correct.

The applicable qualification suite will then establish the qualification state of the modified implementation.

Human Authority

Automation may execute engineering processes where authorized.

Automation does not replace human authority at decision boundaries established by policy.

Automated build, validation, qualification, testing, evidence collection, integration, or processing does not independently grant:

- Approval.
- Authority.
- Qualification beyond demonstrated evidence.
- Release authorization.
- Policy authority.
- Expansion of project scope.

Human authority remains applicable wherever required by established STN-LABZ policy.

Engineering Principle

Development is continuous and evidence-driven.

Qualification is evidence of demonstrated behavior, not an assumption of correctness.

Qualification establishes the demonstrated state of a specific implementation at a specific point in the development cycle.

It does not terminate engineering development.

Modification of a qualified implementation returns that implementation to the applicable development and qualification process.

Previous qualification is not inherited by a modified implementation.

Failures discovered during qualification are engineering findings.

Regressions are corrected, not waived.

Operational evidence may establish new requirements and initiate the next development cycle.

The continuing engineering relationship is:

Requirement
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Build Validation
    ↓
Verification
    ↓
Qualification
    ↓
Integration
    ↓
Operation
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Next Development Cycle

Policy Relationship

This doctrine establishes the common STN-LABZ engineering progression.

Applicable established policies govern specialized requirements and boundaries encountered during that progression.

These may include:

- Module creation.
- Core and module qualification.
- Change control.
- Engineering documentation.
- Knowledge approval.
- Network isolation.
- AI-assisted development.
- Security research.
- Project-specific engineering requirements.

Where a specialized policy applies, that policy governs the applicable activity without replacing the overall engineering development cycle.

The engineering development cycle does not grant authority beyond applicable policy, approved requirements, assigned mission, or human authority.

Evidence Basis

This doctrine is informed by observed STN-LABZ development and qualification activity.

The Rictus Core qualification effort demonstrated that:

- Qualification tests can expose defects not apparent during ordinary successful operation.
- Qualification remains incomplete until all applicable mandatory tests have successfully executed.
- Later development may introduce regressions into previously passing behavior.
- Regression failures require correction before qualification proceeds.
- Negative and adversarial validation provide engineering evidence unavailable through positive-path testing alone.
- The full applicable qualification bench establishes the authoritative qualification result after development changes.
- Qualification applies to the specific implementation revision represented by the successful qualification evidence.
- Future modified revisions do not automatically inherit previous qualification.

Development and architecture reports may provide additional engineering evidence as projects continue through subsequent development cycles.

Proposed architectures remain proposed until approved through the applicable STN-LABZ process.
