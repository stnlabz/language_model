TOPIC:
- Operational Authority Model

OBJECTIVE:
- Define a scalable operational authority model for all STN-LABZ systems.

DEFINITION:
- Reject traditional RBAC as the primary authority model.

PRINCIPLES:
- Tier defines operational depth.
- Certification defines qualification.
- Assignment defines mission responsibility.
- All actions are auditable.
- Human authority remains the final decision maker.

SUMMARY:
- Operational responsibility will be determined by authority, qualification, and assignment, rather than a collection of individual permissions.
