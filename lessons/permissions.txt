Permissions define specific authority granted to a principal, role, group, service, process, or other recognized subject within a system.

A permission answers a narrow question:

Is this subject allowed to perform this action within this scope?

Permissions are part of authorization.

Permissions are not authentication.

Authentication establishes identity.

Permissions describe authority.

Authorization evaluates whether the established authority permits the requested operation.

Keep these concepts separate.

A permission should describe an actual operation.
