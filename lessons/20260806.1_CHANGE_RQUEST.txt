Purpose

The Change Request (CR) process provides a standardized method for proposing, reviewing, approving, implementing, and preserving the history of changes to established STN-LABZ systems and controlled assets.

A Change Request documents what is being changed, why the change is necessary, what may be affected, and who authorized the change.

Policy

A material change to an established STN-LABZ system or controlled asset will require an approved Change Request before implementation unless another approved policy establishes a more specific change process.

A Change Request does not itself authorize activity outside existing organizational authority, policy, qualification requirements, or mission boundaries.

Approval of a Change Request authorizes the described change to proceed within its approved scope.

Applicability

A Change Request may apply to changes involving:

- System architecture.
- Core behavior.
- Module behavior.
- Operational behavior.
- Interfaces.
- Security controls.
- Data structures or persistent state.
- Configuration baselines.
- Approved dependencies.
- Qualification requirements.
- Controlled engineering processes.
- Other established assets where the change materially affects approved behavior, operation, trust, or architecture.

Routine maintenance that does not materially alter approved behavior may be performed without a Change Request unless otherwise required by applicable policy.

Typographical corrections, formatting corrections, documentation cleanup, and other non-substantive editorial changes do not require a Change Request.

A new module is governed by the Module Creation Request (MCR) process and will not use a Change Request as a substitute for an MCR.

Approval Boundary

A Change Request remains Pending until reviewed by the appropriate human authority.

A Rejected Change Request does not authorize implementation.

An Approved Change Request authorizes only the change and scope described in the approved request.

Material expansion beyond the approved scope requires additional human review and, when applicable, a new or revised Change Request.

Implementation does not establish successful qualification.

Qualification does not expand the authority granted by the Change Request.

Historical Record

Change Requests form part of the engineering history of the affected STN-LABZ asset.

Approved, rejected, failed, and reverted Change Requests will be preserved according to applicable STN-LABZ engineering documentation requirements.

A rejected or unsuccessful Change Request will not be removed merely because the proposed change was not adopted.
